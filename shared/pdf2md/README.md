# pdf2md

The **pdf2md** working group turns PDFs into structured markdown an LLM can probe deeply. This folder onboards people considering joining.

## Contents

**`pdf2md-onboarding-brief.md`** — the synthesis brief. It answers three questions, numbered Q1–Q3 in the document:

1. What a PDF is, why we have it, and what it was optimized for.
2. The challenges of converting PDFs into structured markdown, and the state of the art — open-source and paid.
3. The pipeline we would build: convert a directory of PDFs by recipe, emitting YAML-tagged markdown.

It also includes a synthesis, open questions, and a two-part toolkit-paper outline — **pdf2md** (convert and own the markdown) and **pdf2mk** (managed knowledge via PaperQA2/Elicit).

**`pdf-llm-sources.bib`** — eight curated, Zotero-ready sources grounding the brief.
